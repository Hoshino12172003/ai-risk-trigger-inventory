# Provisional Research Questions

These research questions are provisional. They will be frozen only after the
formal data audit.

## RQ1

Are reconfiguration triggers explained only by risk-control parameters such as
Gamma and beta, or also by demand, inventory and network structure?

## RQ2

Can a learning model generalize trigger decisions across previously unseen
networks or empirical decision contexts?

## RQ3

Can a learning-based risk trigger reduce unnecessary exact reoptimization while
controlling economically important missed triggers?
